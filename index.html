📖 What these dimensions mean
🎯Market Need
Is there a real, unsolved problem? Can you name who has it? A strong score here means you understand your customer before you've written a business plan.
🧫Scientific Evidence
Do your results hold up? Have others seen them? Investors trust your science before they trust your business. Evidence is credibility.
🗺️Business Groundwork
Who owns your IP? What will the next phase cost? Groundwork is everything outside the science, and it trips up most scientist-founders.
🔥Founder Drive
Building a company is different from running a lab. Drive measures whether you want that shift and have bandwidth for it. It's the fuel the other three dimensions run on.
